About

Forge 11 is a research project by Sulaiman Waheeduddin.

About me

I'm Sulaiman Waheeduddin, a first-year finance student at Marquette University and a member of a Marquette investment club. I was homeschooled and took dual-enrollment courses at BYU-Idaho, and I've worked as an independent math and English tutor since 2022. I built Forge 11 to learn how quantitative research really works, and especially how easy it is for a backtest to fool you. What interests me most isn't finding a formula that makes money, but learning how to tell a real result from a lucky one. I'm hoping to join the Marquette AIM program to keep building those skills alongside people who share my interest in investing.

About the project

The aim is a credible method rather than an impressive backtest: weak or negative results are reported as they are, failed runs and bugs stay in the record, and the plan, the hypotheses and the rules for the final test were committed before the runs they govern. The language models run locally on the author's computer.

Disclaimer

This is an educational research project, not investment advice. All results are simulated backtests, include assumptions that real trading would not match exactly, and do not predict future returns.
